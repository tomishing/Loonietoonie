LoonieToonie

LoonieToonie is a household expense app. Scan your receipts, link your bank and credit card accounts, and see where your money actually goes: by category, by store, and month by month.

What it does

  • Scan receipts. The text is read on your phone; the app turns it into items and categories.
  • Link bank and card accounts through Plaid, so purchases without a receipt are counted too.
  • See patterns. Spending by category and store, compared with your usual month.
  • Ask questions about your spending in plain language.

Your data stays yours

Your receipts and transactions are saved in a spreadsheet and a folder in your own Google Drive. LoonieToonie can only see the files it created there. It has no database of your spending, it shows no ads, and it never sells data.

LoonieToonie is a personal project, currently used by a single household. Privacy policy · Source code
