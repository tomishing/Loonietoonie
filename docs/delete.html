Delete your data

LoonieToonie Tracker has no separate account and no database of your spending. You sign in with Google, and your receipts, photos, transactions, Ask conversations, and settings are saved in a LoonieToonie folder in your own Google Drive. So you delete them yourself, right away, without waiting for anyone.

Delete everything

  1. Linked banks (web app only): if you linked a bank or card through Plaid, open Account and tap Unlink on each one. This deletes its access key from the LoonieToonie server. The Android app never links banks, so most people can skip this step.
  2. Your data: in Google Drive, delete the LoonieToonie folder, then empty the Drive trash (Drive otherwise keeps deleted files for 30 days).
  3. LoonieToonie's access: at myaccount.google.com/permissions, choose LoonieToonie Tracker and remove its access. This ends your account: LoonieToonie can no longer read or create anything in your Drive.
  4. On your phone: sign out on the Account page, or uninstall the app. Your sign-in is kept only on your device and goes with it.

Delete only some data

  • A receipt: open it and tap Delete.
  • An imported statement: Account → Accounts & cards → tap Import history under the account → Undo next to the file.
  • An Ask conversation: Ask → History → the trash icon next to it.
  • Your household type and province: Account → Your household → Clear.

These remove the data from the app, but the spreadsheet keeps each row, marked deleted = TRUE, so that your other devices notice the change. To erase a row for good, delete it in your LoonieToonie spreadsheet in Google Drive (the receipts, items, bank_transactions, and chat_messages tabs), and delete a receipt's photo from the receipts folder inside the LoonieToonie folder.

What the server keeps

Nothing of your spending. Server logs record which parts of the app were used and when, with your Google account ID but no receipt or transaction contents. If you'd like anything connected to your account removed from them, or need help deleting your data, email loonietoonietracker.dev@gmail.com.
