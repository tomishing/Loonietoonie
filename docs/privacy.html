Privacy policy

Effective October 30, 2026

LoonieToonie Tracker (“LoonieToonie”) is a household expense app. This policy explains what information it uses, where that information is kept, and who else receives it. In short: your spending data lives in your own Google Drive, LoonieToonie keeps no database of it, and it is never sold or used for ads.

Google account and Drive

You sign in with Google. LoonieToonie asks for these permissions only:

  • Your name, email address, and profile picture, to show who is signed in.
  • Access to files LoonieToonie creates in your Google Drive (the drive.file permission). It cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.

With that permission, LoonieToonie creates a LoonieToonie folder and spreadsheet in your Drive. Your receipts, their items, receipt photos, imported bank and card transactions, your categories, and your settings (including the household type and province you choose for comparing with similar households) are saved there. You can open, edit, export, or delete them in Google Drive at any time.

LoonieToonie's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Receipts

  • The text on a receipt photo or PDF is read on your device.
  • That text is sent to Anthropic (Claude) to turn it into a store, date, items, and categories. The photo itself is sent only if the text alone could not be read correctly, once.
  • After you save a receipt, its photo is stored in your Google Drive folder.

Bank and credit card accounts

  • You can import a statement file you download from your bank's website (CSV, OFX, or QFX). The file is read on the LoonieToonie server and not kept; its transactions are saved in your Google Drive spreadsheet. LoonieToonie never signs in to your bank for this.
  • To suggest categories, the names of merchants the app hasn't seen before (for example "SAFEWAY #4501 BURNABY BC") are sent to Anthropic (Claude), without amounts, dates, or account numbers.
  • Linking a bank (web app, invited households only). The Android app doesn't link banks. On the web app, households invited to try it can link an account instead of importing statements; the points below apply only to them.
  • If you link an account, you sign in to your bank through Plaid. Your bank username and password go to Plaid, never to LoonieToonie. See Plaid's End User Privacy Policy.
  • Plaid gives LoonieToonie an access key for the linked accounts. It is stored encrypted on the LoonieToonie server, never in your spreadsheet, and is used only to import your transactions.
  • Imported transactions (date, merchant, amount, category) are saved in your Google Drive spreadsheet.
  • Unlinking an account revokes LoonieToonie's access at Plaid and deletes its access key from the server.

Ask (spending questions)

When you ask a question in Ask, your question and the spending figures needed to answer it (totals, categories, stores, and purchases from your spreadsheet) are sent to Anthropic to write the answer. Your conversations are saved in your own LoonieToonie spreadsheet, so you can come back to them; you can delete them in Ask (History) or in the spreadsheet. The LoonieToonie server does not keep them.

What the LoonieToonie server keeps

Information Kept?
Receipts, transactions, photos, categories, Ask conversations No. They are in your Google Drive. The server reads them briefly to answer the app's requests.
Plaid access keys for linked accounts Yes, encrypted, until you unlink the account.
Your sign-in Kept on your device as an encrypted session, not in a server database.
Server logs Which parts of the app were used and when, not receipt or transaction contents.

Who receives your information

  • Google, which stores your spreadsheet and photos in your Drive.
  • Anthropic, which reads receipt text (and occasionally a photo), suggests categories for merchant names from imported statements, and answers Ask questions.
  • Plaid, which connects to your bank when you link an account (web app, invited households only).
  • Fly.io, which hosts the LoonieToonie server in Toronto, Canada.

Each handles data under its own terms. LoonieToonie does not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone else, does not show ads, and does not use your data for advertising.

Deleting your data

  • Unlink your bank and card accounts on the Account page; this deletes their access keys from the server.
  • Delete Ask conversations in Ask's History.
  • Delete the LoonieToonie folder from your Google Drive to remove your receipts, transactions, and conversations.
  • Remove LoonieToonie's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions can be sent through the project's GitHub page.
